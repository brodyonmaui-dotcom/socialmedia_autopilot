Privacy policy

This is a private, single-user tool that manages the operator's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Data processed: the operator's own posts, their insights, and comments and direct messages sent to the operator's accounts, used only to schedule posts, reply, and report performance. Data is stored on servers controlled by the operator, is not sold or shared, and is deleted on request or when the operator disconnects an account.

Data deletion requests: send a direct message to the operator's account, and the related data will be deleted within 30 days.
