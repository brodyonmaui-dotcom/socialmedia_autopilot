# Brief: inbox triage recalibration, promo-spam bucket, approve-from-notification (backend + data layer)

Read `docs/prep/run-4-agent-rules.md` (binding), `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/voice.md` ("Comment replies"), `docs/runbooks/` engagement runbook. You do not commit. This is safety-relevant code: the lead reads every line of the diff.

## Problems
1. Almost every comment is flagged. Example cause: `packages/engagement/src/rules.ts` `LOCATION` contains bare words such as "gym", "near", "meet", "tomorrow", "park", so "love the gym content" is flagged as a location question.
2. Promo-spam ("love it 🔥 send it to @somepage", "DM us to be featured", paid-promotion offers in DMs) lands in the flagged inbox. He gets many. They must be ignored: no reply, no flag, hidden from the main inbox, visible under an "Ignored" filter.
3. End goal: his phone pings "X commented …, drafted reply …, approve?" and one tap sends it.

## Safety frame (do not weaken)
- Shadow mode stays the default per platform. Nothing in this task turns on auto-send. A promo-spam item never gets a reply of any kind (auto or drafted) and never a "hide" action.
- Rules may still only make the model's output more cautious. The new bucket is the most cautious one for replying (never reply).
- Location, brand/business and real questions stay flagged. The recalibration removes false flags on plain praise only. Replies never reveal whereabouts; the post-rules lint on drafted replies is unchanged or stricter.
- One-tap "Approve & send" goes through the existing manual-send gates (kill switch, limits, blocklist, platform capability, idempotency / unknown-state handling). No new send path.

2. **Rules** (`packages/engagement/src/rules.ts`, pure, tests first):
   - `promoRule(text, author, promoAccounts)` runs **before** everything else and before the model. Hits: author on the promo-account list; "send (it|this) to @x", "dm (us|@x) (to|for) (be )?featured|promo|collab", "promote (it|this|your …) (on|at|with) @x", "get featured", "we('d| would) love to feature you", "paid promo(tion)", "shoutout for shoutout|s4s", "check (out )?our page", growth/follower-selling phrasing, and the generic "<praise> + send/DM/submit to @handle" shape. Conservative: a comment that merely mentions another @handle in conversation ("@friend look at this") is NOT promo. Return `{ bucket: 'spam_promo', reason: '<rule id>' }`.
   - Precedence: HIDE rules (threats, doxxing, scams) still win over promo (they must reach him). Promo wins over BUSINESS and LINK for the promo shapes, so a "DM us for promo" no longer flags.
   - Recalibrate LOCATION: flag only whereabouts **questions or requests**: "where …", "which/what gym", "what city/town", "your address/location", "are you in/near/from …", "come train/meet", "can we meet", times-and-places requests ("when are you there", "what time do you train"), plus the F7 red-team phrasings already listed, the ADDRESS and ZIP shapes and the creator's blocklist. Bare topical words ("gym", "park", "beach", "trail", "city", "tomorrow", "tonight", "near", "visit", "schedule", "meet") no longer flag by themselves.
   - Recalibrate BUSINESS the same way: keep real business approaches ("collab?", "sponsor", "rate card", "do you offer coaching", "signals group", "telegram/whatsapp" + link shape); bare "brand", "business", "pricing", "promo" inside ordinary praise or trading chat don't flag alone.
   - Questions: any real question about him or the content (ends with "?" or starts with an interrogative) that the model can't answer from the allowed pools stays `flag`. Rhetorical praise ("how are you this good?!", "bro what 🔥?") may be `simple_positive` only if the model says so with confidence ≥ MIN_CONFIDENCE; the pre-rules don't decide it.

4. **Pipeline**: `spam_promo` comments get a triage row and nothing else (no engagement_actions row at all).
