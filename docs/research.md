### Duplicate content, hashtags and AI-text rules: IG, TikTok, YouTube (2026-11-28, docs only; F26 B)
Sources: creators.instagram.com/blog/recommendations-and-originality, creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram, help.instagram.com/351460621611097, help.instagram.com/1013292530224018, tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/fyf-standards, support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658, support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392. Code: `packages/core/src/{judge,hashtags,caption-compose}.ts`, `packages/research/src/auto/caption-ai.ts`. Not exercised live.
- Instagram: identical content is deduplicated in recommendations, only the original is recommended; accounts that mostly post unoriginal content lose recommendations (rolling 30 days, post of 2026-04-30); trial reels get limited reach when the content was shared before; <= 5 hashtags (hard limit). Self-reposts: **no explicit rule [INF: treated as a duplicate]**.
- TikTok: unoriginal or re-uploaded content without new edits, and content carrying other apps' watermarks, is not eligible for the For You feed. Hashtag count or frequency: **nothing official**; 3-5 is our choice [INF].
- YouTube: only the first 3 hashtags are shown; with more than 60 all hashtags are ignored; unrelated hashtags and repetitive description sentences are prohibited; "inauthentic content" = interchangeable, template-like videos.
- Our rules built on these: judge blocks IG > 5 and YouTube > 60 hashtags, warns YouTube > 3; warns on a hashtag set equal to one of the last 5 posts and on captions with word-trigram Jaccard >= 0.6 to the last 30 days (blocks at >= 0.8) [INF thresholds]. Duration bounds in the judge (IG/TikTok 3-180 s, YouTube 1-180 s) are **[INF]** warnings only.
- AI captions: model `claude-haiku-5-5`, text only (hashtags come from the bank), monthly cap 500 cents in `settings.ai_caption_spend_<YYYY-MM>`. Price per token assumed ($1 / $5 per million in / out) **[INF, check against the Anthropic price list]**.
